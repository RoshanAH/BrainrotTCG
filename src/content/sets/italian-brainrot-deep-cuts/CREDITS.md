# Italian Brainrot Deep Cuts: image credits

These are the real meme images, used as card art. Most Italian brainrot characters are AI-generated images first posted on
TikTok, so each "creator" below is the TikTok account that first posted the character. The Steal a Brainrot Wiki records
these accounts and hosts the original images, since the Italian Brainrot Wiki blocks automated access. I downloaded each image once,
checked it with Pillow, re-encoded it to PNG (which strips metadata) and cropped it to the art window's 6:7 portrait
shape. Code adds the frame, title and caption. All rights stay with the owners listed below. The set is still a draft.

## Rarity changes

The old file ranked the characters backwards: Lirilì Larilà, one of the first-wave icons that Wikipedia and Know Your Meme name next to
Tralalero Tralala and Bombardiro Crocodilo, was a COMMON. The new order puts the most famous characters in the rarest slots.

- BRAINROT: Lirilì Larilà
- LEGENDARY: Strawberry Elephant (a meme since the 2020s and Steal a Brainrot's first OG)
- EPIC: Trippi Troppi (Wikipedia lists it among the core characters, and Know Your Meme has an entry for it)
- RARE: Dragon Cannelloni and Pot Hotspot
- UNCOMMON: Tric Trac Baraboom, Glorbo Fruttodrillo and Chicleteira
- COMMON: Spijuniro Golubiro, Trulimero Trulicina, Ballerino Lololo and Gangster Footera

## Replaced cards

Seven of the original 12 characters are claimed by **Mementum Lab**, a French agency, and its licensing brand **Brainrot Factory**.
The agency is actively enforcing those claims. It sent cease-and-desist letters to Steal a Brainrot's developers and to Epic Games. Tung Tung Tung
Sahur was pulled from Steal a Brainrot in September 2025, and Epic removed the brainrot characters from Fortnite until it signed a deal with Mementum.
Mementum is also in a US federal case (Spyder Games v. Mementum Lab, N.D. Cal., filed November 2025), where in June 2026 it filed trademark
counterclaims on behalf of the creators it represents, Noxa and Breno (@ofuscabreno). The Italian Brainrot Wiki reports that some Roblox games removed
Brr Brr Patapim after Mementum registered it. Mementum also represents @alexey_pigeon. That evidence of takedowns and lawsuits rules out these cards:

The replacements are the most famous 2025 Italian brainrot and Steal a Brainrot characters whose creators aren't linked to Mementum and
that aren't in Genesis: Tric Trac Baraboom, Glorbo Fruttodrillo, Pot Hotspot (Know Your Meme "Hotspot Bro"), Spijuniro Golubiro (has its own Know Your Meme entry),
Trulimero Trulicina (Know Your Meme), Ballerino Lololo and Gangster Footera. I skipped these:

- Udin Din Din Dun, Esok Sekolah and Garama and Madundung, because their creator is Noxa (Mementum).
- Girafa Celestre, Il Cacto Hipopotamo, Graipuss Medussi, Spaghetti Tualetti and Tim Cheese, because they are Mementum creators' characters.
- La Grande Combinasion and Los Combinasionas, because they are mash-ups of Mementum characters.
- Los Tralaleritos and Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur, because they are spin-offs of the Genesis cards Tralalero Tralala and Tung Tung Tung Sahur.
- Svinina Bombardino and Nuclearo Dinossauro, because their only images I could find are under 400 px.

One kept character was renamed: the old card's "Chicleteira Bicicleta" is now Chicleteira (full name "Chicleteira Bicicleteira", see above).
