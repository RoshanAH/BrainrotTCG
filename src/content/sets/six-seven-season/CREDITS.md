# Six Seven Season: image credits

The mainstream memes of 2025, apart from Italian brainrot, which has its own set. Each card shows the real meme image. I
downloaded each image once on 2026-10-12 into its own empty folder and checked it with Pillow. I then re-encoded it to
PNG, which strips metadata, and cropped it to the art window's 6:7 portrait shape. Code adds the frame, title and caption.
The owners listed below keep all rights. The set is still a draft.

Each meme was confirmed as genuinely big in 2025 by at least two independent sources, listed in the notes. The rarest
slots follow Know Your Meme's user-voted "Top 20 Memes of 2025" (Dec 17, 2025,
https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/meme-review/meme-of-the-year-the-top-20-memes-of-2025), its 2025 Meme of the Month
polls and mainstream news coverage.

## Rarity order

The most iconic memes of the year hold the rarest slots:

- BRAINROT: 6-7 (Dictionary.com's Word of the Year; #1 in KYM's year poll with 27.5%)
- LEGENDARY: Chicken Jockey (April's Meme of the Month with 38.3%; #8 of the year; cinema chaos worldwide)
- EPIC: Jet2 Holiday (#11 of the year; NBC News, 1.2M+ TikToks, UK-born and global)
- RARE: 100 Men vs 1 Gorilla (May's Meme of the Month; a 70M-view tweet) and Montoya Por Favor (a Spanish reality TV clip; one repost passed 219M views, and brands and sports clubs worldwide joined in)
- UNCOMMON: James Doakes (#5 of the year), Jon Hamm Dancing (December's Meme of the Month) and Dubai Chocolate (a global food craze that caused a pistachio shortage)
- COMMON: Played These Games (January's Meme of the Month), Penguin Tariffs, Frieren Looking Up (#14 of the year) and Ashton Hall's Routine (#2 in March)
