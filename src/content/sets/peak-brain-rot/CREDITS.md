# Peak Brain Rot: image credits

The set is named after Oxford's 2024 Word of the Year, "brain rot". Each card shows the real meme image. I
downloaded each one once on 2026-10-12 into its own empty folder and checked it with Pillow. I then re-encoded
it to PNG, which strips metadata, and cropped it to the art window's 6:7 portrait shape. Code adds the frame,
title and caption. The owners listed below keep all rights. The set is still a draft.

Each meme was checked as genuinely big in 2024 against Know Your Meme (KYM) and at least one other publisher,
with dates in the notes. KYM's monthly "Meme of the Month" (MOTM) polls and its "Top 20 Memes of 2024" reader
poll (https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/poll/kym-review-the-top-20-memes-of-2024, Dec 13, 2024) count as KYM.

## Considered and skipped

- **Owner enforces or has sued:** Chill Guy (creator Phillip Banks enforces), Moo Deng (the zoo trademarked her),
  Elmo's "How is everybody doing?" (Sesame Workshop has sued over Sesame parody marketing), KSI's "Thick of It"
  (copyright-strike complaints, and the only image is KSI himself), Very Demure (trademark filings and a dispute
  over the phrase; the image is the TikToker herself).
- **Private individuals or minors:** Hawk Tuah Girl, Tiny Green Mall Wizard, the Four Seasons Orlando baby, the
  Rizzler and Big Justice, the Carnival rizz party, lookalike contests, Gen Z Boss and a Mini.
- **Tone or content:** Brazilian Miku (KYM #2 of 2024, but the fan art sexualises a character whose official age is 16),
  the Dune 2 popcorn bucket (KYM files it under "Fleshlight"; the jokes are sexual), Raygun (the memes became
  harassment), Jigsaw's Final Challenge (Saw), Let's Go Gambling (a gambling joke in a pack-opening game),
  Cursed Plankton (moaning audio), Luigi Mangione, Diddy memes, and the election memes (Kamala's coconut tree,
  Trump at McDonald's, the JD Vance couch hoax), which I left out to keep the set non-partisan.
- **No usable real image:** That Feeling When Knee Surgery Is Tomorrow (Nov 2024 MOTM, 2nd; the only blue Grinch
  copies are blurry screenshots with an ad caption burned in), Galvanized Square Steel (May 2024 MOTM winner;
  KYM's covers are collages of TikTokers), the CrowdStrike outage (a blue screen in an airport, with real harm
  behind it).
- **Other:** Absolute Cinema (KYM dates it to 2023, and the image is a New York Times photo), Chipi Chipi Chapa
  Chapa (mostly a 2023 meme), Kim Ye-ji (would be a fourth Paris 2024 card).
