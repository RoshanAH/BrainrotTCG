(which strips metadata) and cropped to the art window's 6:7 portrait shape. The frame, title and caption
are added by code. All rights stay with the owners listed below. The set is still a draft.

## Needs a Gemini lookalike

These cards keep their SVG placeholder. Their `art` brief in `set.json` describes the lookalike to make
(see `PROMPTS.md`). None of them has a downloaded image.

| Card | Why it was skipped |
| --- | --- |
| Keyboard Cat (`keyboard-cat`) | The owner's list says to skip it. Creator Charlie Schmidt licenses the character and sued Warner Bros. and 5th Cell over its use in *Scribblenauts* (2013). |
| Planking (`planking`) | Every well-known planking photo (KYM's header, the Wikipedia and Commons photos) shows a private individual. The one public-figure image is rugby player David "Wolfman" Williams' 2011 try celebration (https://i.kym-cdn.com/photos/images/original/000/126/842/Wolfman-Plank1.jpg). It's a 650×366 landscape press photo, and a 6:7 portrait crop would cut him in half. The brief asks for a faceless planker. |
| Trollface (`trollface`) | The owner's list says to skip it. Creator Carlos Ramirez registered the copyright and enforces it; for example, the game *Meme Run* was pulled from the Wii U eShop in 2015 after his claim. |
| Nyan Cat (`nyan-cat`) | The owner's list says to skip it. Creator Chris Torres joined the 2013 *Scribblenauts* lawsuit against Warner Bros. and licenses the character. |
| Rickroll (`rickroll`) | The owner's list says to skip it. The meme is a real person, Rick Astley, who has enforced his likeness (in 2023 he sued Yung Gravy over an imitation of his voice). The brief is a faceless silhouette. |
