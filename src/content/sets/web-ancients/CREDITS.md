## Rarity changes

- **Gangnam Style** moved up from RARE to LEGENDARY. It was the first video to reach 1 billion YouTube views, which makes
  it the most famous meme left in the set once Nyan Cat was replaced.
- The BRAINROT slot went to **I Can Has Cheezburger?**. Its 2007 caption started LOLcats and the Cheezburger network,
  and the set's tagline ("LOL, I can has pack?") already refers to it.
- **Trololo Guy** took the RARE slot that Gangnam Style left. **Forever Alone** took Trollface's EPIC slot, and **Me Gusta**
  and **Double Rainbow** took the two COMMON slots that Keyboard Cat and Planking left.

## Replaced cards

These memes were removed because they can't use their real image. Each replacement went into the slot shown, at the same rarity.

| Removed card | Slot | Replaced by | Why it was removed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keyboard Cat (`keyboard-cat`) | COMMON | Me Gusta | The owner enforces it. Creator Charlie Schmidt licenses the character and sued Warner Bros. and 5th Cell over its use in *Scribblenauts* (2013). |
| Planking (`planking`) | COMMON | Double Rainbow | Every well-known planking photo (KYM's header, the Wikipedia and Commons photos) is of a private individual. The one public-figure image is rugby player David "Wolfman" Williams' 2011 try celebration (https://i.kym-cdn.com/photos/images/original/000/126/842/Wolfman-Plank1.jpg). It's a 650×366 landscape press photo, and a 6:7 portrait crop would cut him in half. |
| Trollface (`trollface`) | EPIC | Forever Alone | The owner enforces it. Creator Carlos Ramirez registered the copyright and enforces it; for example, the game *Meme Run* was pulled from the Wii U eShop in 2015 after his claim. |
| Nyan Cat (`nyan-cat`) | LEGENDARY | Gangnam Style (moved up from RARE); its RARE slot went to Trololo Guy | The owner enforces it. Creator Chris Torres joined the 2013 *Scribblenauts* lawsuit against Warner Bros. and licenses the character. |
| Rickroll (`rickroll`) | BRAINROT | I Can Has Cheezburger? | The meme is built on a real person, Rick Astley, who has enforced his likeness (in 2023 he sued Yung Gravy over an imitation of his voice). |

Suggested replacements I didn't use:

- **It's Over 9000!** KYM dates it to 2006, which is before this set's era. Toei Animation, which owns *Dragon Ball*, also
  has a record of mass takedowns of fan videos built on its anime (in December 2021 it filed 150+ claims against the
  reviewer Totally Not Mark: https://torrentfreak.com/?p=212470). Gangnam Style is more famous anyway, so it takes LEGENDARY.
- **This Is Sparta!** KYM also lists it as 2006, and the only image KYM has is 350×259.
- **O RLY?** (2003) and **All Your Base Are Belong to Us** (2001) are both earlier than this set's 2007–2012 era.
