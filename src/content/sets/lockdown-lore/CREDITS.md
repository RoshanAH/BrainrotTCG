# Lockdown Lore: image credits

These are real meme images, used as card art. I downloaded each one once, checked it with Pillow, re-encoded
it to PNG (which strips metadata) and cropped it to the art window's 6:7 portrait shape. Code adds the frame,
title and caption. All rights stay with the owners listed below. The set is still a draft.

## Replaced cards

- **You're On Mute** (COMMON) was replaced. The images people know for it are screenshots of real video calls full of
  private people, and there is no famous people-free image of it.
- **It's Corn** (RARE) was replaced. Every image of the meme centres on Tariq, the boy from the 2022 interview, who
  was 7 at the time. A generic corn cob photo would be a lookalike, not the meme.
- In their place: **Coffin Dance** (2020) and **Bernie's Mittens** (2021). These are probably the two most famous memes
  of the era that weren't already in the game. Among Us / "sus" was skipped because Genesis already has Amogus.

## Renamed cards

Parody names were changed to the real meme names: Lord Yeastington → **Sourdough Starter**, Whipped Dalgona →
**Dalgona Coffee**, Bing Chilling Cone → **Bing Chilling**, I'm Not a Kitten → **I'm Not a Cat**, Ever Stuck →
**Ever Given**, Toilet Paper King → **Toilet Paper Panic**. Their ids changed to match. The set is unreleased, so
there are no saved collections to break.

## Rarity changes

The rarest slots now go to the most famous memes:

- BRAINROT: **Coffin Dance**, the defining meme of the 2020 lockdown. The pallbearers became pandemic mascots
  ("stay at home or dance with us"), and their image was used in stay-home messages around the world.
- LEGENDARY: **Bernie's Mittens**, 2021's most-shared image.
- EPIC: **Bing Chilling**, up from COMMON. It's still everywhere years later.
- RARE: **Toilet Paper Panic** (down from BRAINROT) and **Ever Given** (down from EPIC).
- UNCOMMON: **Big Floppa** (down from LEGENDARY), **Vibing Cat** and **I'm Not a Cat** (down from RARE).
- COMMON: **Stick Bug** (down from UNCOMMON), **Polish Cow** (down from UNCOMMON), **Dalgona Coffee** and **Sourdough Starter**.
