# Right Now: image credits

Each card shows a real meme image. I downloaded each one once on 2026-10-12 and checked it with Pillow. I then
re-encoded it to PNG, which strips metadata, and cropped it to the art window's 6:7 portrait shape. Code adds the
frame, title and caption. The owners listed below keep all rights. The set is still a draft.

Before an image was used, each trend was confirmed as real and current (Jul–Oct 2026) by at least two sources. The
sources are listed in the notes.

**Rarity changes.** I re-slotted rarities by how famous each meme is:

- Jimothy moved from COMMON to BRAINROT. It was KYM's Meme of the Month and has its own Wikipedia article. It also got a Google easter egg, a Fortnite companion and a White House post.
- AI Rumpelstiltskin moved to LEGENDARY.
- Verity moved to EPIC.
- Steal an Egg and Black Cat Clapping moved to RARE.
- Remember November moved to UNCOMMON.
- Bukangi moved to COMMON, because it is mostly known in Korea.
