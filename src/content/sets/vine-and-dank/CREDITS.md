# Vine & Dank: image credits

These are the real meme images used for card art. Each one was downloaded once, checked with Pillow,
re-encoded to PNG (which strips metadata) and cropped to the art window's 6:7 portrait shape. Code adds
the frame, title and caption. All rights stay with the owners listed below. The set is still a draft.

For the ten images from the first pass, I downloaded each listed URL again and matched it against that
pass's download. Eight matched byte for byte. I re-cropped Area 51 from the same file. I replaced the
OK Boomer and All Those Chickens images with copies whose sources I could trace (see their notes).

### How the replacements were picked

- **Woman Yelling at a Cat** has the most KYM views of any usable 2013–2019 meme I checked: 5.4M, against
  Dat Boi's 3.6M and Doge's 14.7M. That's why it takes the BRAINROT slot.
- **Mocking SpongeBob** (2.2M views) is better known than All Those Chickens, which has no KYM entry at all.
  So it took the UNCOMMON slot, and Chickens moved down to the croissant's COMMON slot.
- **Ruled out:**
  - Big Chungus: Warner Bros. filed an EU trademark in Aug 2022 that covers games and toys, and Genesis
    already has a Big Chungus card.
  - Surprised Pikachu: The Pokémon Company is known for frequent takedowns.
  - Harambe and Pepe: both are tied to hate and harassment campaigns.
  - Distracted Boyfriend: the people in the stock photo are private individuals.
