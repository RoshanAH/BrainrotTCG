# Spring Breakdown: image credits

Each card shows a real meme image. I downloaded each one once on 2026-10-12, each into its own empty folder, and checked it with
Pillow. I then re-encoded it to PNG, which strips metadata, and cropped it to the art window's 6:7 portrait shape. Code adds the
frame, title and caption. The owners listed below keep all rights. The set is still a draft.

Before an image was used, each trend was confirmed as real and inside the window (Jan–Jun 2026) by at least two publishers plus a
platform signal. The sources and dates are listed in the notes.

Two images are under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (Punch the Monkey and Dubai Chewy Cookie). Their cards must keep the attribution below, and the
finished cards built from them are adaptations that fall under the same licence.
